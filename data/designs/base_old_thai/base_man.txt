Costume design sheet of ONE complete outfit for a character in an old Thai folk tale (village life in old Siam, about 150-200 years ago): ชายชาวบ้าน, man, 25 years old, ordinary young village man / woodcutter; work clothes, chong kraben with a simple short-sleeved cotton shirt, cloth tied at the waist. Men wear a chong kraben (โจงกระเบน) wrapped and tucked between the legs, with a plain collarless cotton shirt or mor hom style shirt with Chinese knot buttons, or bare chest with a pha khao ma sash; women wear a pha sin / pha thung tube skirt with a modest blouse (sleeved, covered). Handwoven natural-dyed cotton, muted earthy colors, simple and beautiful, believable for village folk, NOT royal, NOT dance costume, no gold unless the character is rich. Barefoot or simple sandals.
