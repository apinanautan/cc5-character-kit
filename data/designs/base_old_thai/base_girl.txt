Costume design sheet of ONE complete outfit for a character in an old Thai folk tale (village life in old Siam, about 150-200 years ago): เด็กหญิงชาวบ้าน, girl, 8 years old, poor village girl; small pha sin tube skirt and a simple short-sleeved cotton blouse. Men wear a chong kraben (โจงกระเบน) wrapped and tucked between the legs, with a plain collarless cotton shirt or mor hom style shirt with Chinese knot buttons, or bare chest with a pha khao ma sash; women wear a pha sin / pha thung tube skirt with a modest blouse (sleeved, covered). Handwoven natural-dyed cotton, muted earthy colors, simple and beautiful, believable for village folk, NOT royal, NOT dance costume, no gold unless the character is rich. Barefoot or simple sandals.
